HOW TO PLAY

Left thumb — touch anywhere on the left half to move (floating joystick). Buttons on the right: 斬 slash, 避 dodge, 技 skill.
Move — WASD or arrow keys. J / Space slash · K / Shift dodge · L / E skill · Esc / P pause.
斬 SLASH — attack. Tap in rhythm or hold for a full combo. The last hit of each combo hits hardest. Slashes cut enemy projectiles from the air.
避 DODGE — roll in your move direction. You're invincible while rolling.
見切り Perfect dodge — dodge just as an attack hits you: time slows, you get a free dodge + skill charge, and your attacks deal x2.5 COUNTER damage for a moment.
Combo — keep landing hits without getting hurt. Every hit adds +0.5% damage & souls (up to x1.5 at 100 hits). Getting hit BREAKS the combo.
技 SKILL — fills as you land hits. Each weapon has its own special.
Rooms — clear monsters to open the gates. Gold = treasure, purple = cursed altar (trade max hearts for power), teal = Tanuki shop (spend souls on healing & relics), red = boss.
👑 Elite monsters wear a crown with a kanji: 盾 Shielded, 爆 Explosive (run when it dies!), 疾 Swift, 幻 Phantom, 分 Splitting. Tougher, but drop double souls.
✦ Shiny monsters glow in rare colors (Jade → Prism). Slay them to fill your Shiny Collection and unlock skins at 10, 50 and 100 (Prism only needs 5, 25 and 50).
